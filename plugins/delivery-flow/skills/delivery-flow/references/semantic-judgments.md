# Delivery judgments

Use the installed `jev` skill for the judgment contract and CLI; reuse it when
already loaded. It owns evidence selection, batching, uncertainty and fallback.

Apply judgments where they change a delivery decision: alternatives in
brainstorm, requirement ambiguity in spec, step/requirement alignment in plan
review, or finding triage during execution. Supply the relevant evidence before
deciding. Do not turn phase names into mandatory ceremonial calls.

If Jev is unavailable, report that once and reason explicitly from evidence.
Tests, exact checks and authorization remain independent requirements.
