## Deviations
- s3: the spec named `LaunchOpts { refuse_finished: bool }`. clippy's `struct_excessive_bools` rejects a fourth bool, so the field is `by: Launcher` (`Human` by default, `Engine` from the launch effect). The behaviour is the same.
- s6: AC-7's refusal case runs against an engine socket that answers `unknown op`, which is a real older engine's reply, instead of a served engine with a pending `Merge`. A served engine cannot be given a pending merge without racing its own reconcile. The pending-`Merge` refusal of `begin` is covered on the real engine in `dispatch_ops_test.rs`. A reply without `tracked` or `stage` (an engine on another protocol, which is now being replaced) also refuses.
